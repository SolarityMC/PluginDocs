sidebar_position: 1
---

- **Minecraft 26.3** or newer.
- **Paper** or **Folia** (Canvas works too).
- **Java 25** or newer.

Optional:

- **WorldGuard**: turns on the safe-zone and warzone rules. Without it, every item works everywhere.
- **PlaceholderAPI**: adds the `%solarityitems_...%` placeholders.
