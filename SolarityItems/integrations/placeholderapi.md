sidebar_position: 2
---

With PlaceholderAPI installed, SolarityItems registers the `solarityitems` expansion at startup. No
download through `/papi ecloud` is needed.

Use it to show cooldowns on a scoreboard, hotbar text or a menu. The full list is on
[Placeholders](../core-reference/placeholders.md).
