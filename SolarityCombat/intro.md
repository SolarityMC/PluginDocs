SolarityCombat keeps PvP on Solarity's GenSMP honest, for **Paper** and **Folia** 26.3. A hit tags
both players for a few seconds. While tagged they can't use commands outside a short allow-list,
can't step back into spawn, and die if they log out. Every kill pays the killer a Kill Token, a real
item they spend at the shopkeepers. Item caps stop anyone eating their way through a fight on a
stack of enchanted golden apples.

## Key features

- **Combat tag**: both sides of PvP damage are tagged, with an action-bar countdown.
- **No escapes**: commands are denied except an allow-list, and tagged players can't walk, ride or
  teleport into a safezone.
- **Fair combat logging**: logging out while tagged kills you, but server kicks (flying check, restarts,
  bans) don't count.
- **Item caps**: limit how many of an item a player may use, e.g. three enchanted golden apples.
- **Kill tokens**: one stackable item per kill, with same-victim cooldowns and same-IP checks.
- **Native Folia support**.
