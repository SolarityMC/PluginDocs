## Server

- Minecraft 26.3 or newer
- Paper or Folia (Canvas works too)
- Java 25 or newer

## Optional plugins

Neither is needed to load.

| Plugin | What it adds |
|---|---|
| WorldGuard | The safezone barrier. Without it, tagged players can walk into spawn freely |
| PlaceholderAPI | The `%solaritycombat_...%` placeholders |
