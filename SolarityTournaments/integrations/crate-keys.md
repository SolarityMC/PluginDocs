The default prizes are ExcellentCrates keys: `crates key give %player% seasonal 3`, `2` and `1`. Any
console command works, so another crate plugin only needs its own command in `prizes`.

ExcellentCrates can't give a key to someone offline, which is why prizes wait for their winner and are
handed over a few seconds after they join. See
[The Weekly Cycle & Prizes](../features-modules/weekly-cycle.md).
