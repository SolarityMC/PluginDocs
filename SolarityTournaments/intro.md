SolarityTournaments runs a new tournament every week on Solarity's GenSMP, for **Paper** and **Folia**
26.3. Three boards run at once: Player Kills, Blocks Mined and Money Earned. When the week ends, the
top 3 of every board win Seasonal Keys, the results go into the history menus, and the next week starts
by itself.

## Key features

- **Three boards**: kills, generator blocks mined and money earned, each with its own prizes if you want.
- **Hands-off weeks**: the week ends on a fixed day and time, pays out, and starts the next one.
- **Prizes that always arrive**: each prize is written down when the week ends and handed out exactly once.
- **Fair play built in**: kill-trading, alts and money between players never count.
- **Staff tools**: reset one player's week or disqualify them from every tournament.
- **Native Folia support**, with SQLite off the game threads.
