sidebar_position: 2
---

With PlaceholderAPI installed, SolarityGens registers its expansion twice: as `solaritygens` and as
`dytchsgens`, its name before the rename. Both give the same values, so scoreboards, tab lists and
holograms written for DytchsGens keep working with no changes.

The full list is on the [Placeholders](../core-reference/placeholders.md) page. No PlaceholderAPI
expansion download is needed; it comes with the plugin.
