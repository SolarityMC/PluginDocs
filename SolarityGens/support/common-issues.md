| Message or symptom | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Generators go inside your claim" | Placed outside any claim | Place it inside your claim, or `/gens start` to get one |
| "You can only place generators in a claim you belong to" | Someone else's claim | Use your own claim, or be added as a member |
| "You've used all N of your generator slots" | Slot limit | `/gens expand`, or pick a gen up |
| "This claim already holds N generators" | The claim's own limit (for teams) | Expand the claim |
| "That generator is already placed somewhere else" | A copied gen item | Nothing; the original gen is safe |
| "This generator item isn't recognised" | A forged item, or one from another server | Ask staff; it is in the audit log |
| "You need a ... generator at level 3 first" | Unlock requirement | Upgrade that gen to the level shown |
| "Nothing to collect yet" | The gen was just emptied | Wait a cycle |
| A gen that can't be broken | All gens are protected | Use **Pick Up** in its menu |
| `/autosell` does nothing | No ShopGUIPlus, no economy, or the item has no ShopGUIPlus price | See [ShopGUIPlus](../integrations/shopguiplus.md) |
| "Claim gens are off right now" | The database failed at startup | See [Troubleshooting](../advanced-optimization/troubleshooting.md) |
