## Do my DytchsGens data, items and permissions carry over?

Yes. See [Upgrading from DytchsGens](../getting-started/installation.md#upgrading-from-dytchsgens).

## Do gens produce while I'm offline?

Yes, up to the storage cap (30 minutes of output by default). Logging back in after a week gives the
same as logging in after half an hour.

## Why did my gen stop?

It is full. Collect and sell, and it starts again from that moment.

## Does tiering up replace my old gens?

No. Unlocking a tier gives one gen of the new type and leaves your existing gens alone.

## Can my claim members use my gens?

They can see whose gen it is, but only the player who placed a gen can collect, sell, upgrade or pick
it up.

## Do booster vouchers work on claim gens?

No. Vouchers are for click-to-mine gens. Claim gens only get the [server booster](../features-modules/boosters.md#server-booster).

## Can I move a gen?

Yes: right-click it and click **Pick Up**. You get the same gen back as an item, with its level.

## Is the LandClaim plugin used for claims?

Yes, by default (`claims.provider: landclaim`). See [LandClaim](../integrations/landclaim.md).

## What happens to my gens if my claim is deleted?

They come back to you as items, with their level, and what they had stored is sold to you. If you
were offline you get them when you next join. The same happens to your gens when you leave someone
else's claim.
