SolarityMilestones gives every player a ladder of season milestones on Solarity's GenSMP, for **Paper**
and **Folia** 26.3. Four categories run at once: Kills, Blocks Mined, Playtime and Store Support. Each
one has its own tiers, and every tier pays out a reward the player claims from the `/milestones` menu.
At the end of a season one command wipes everyone back to zero.

## Key features

- **Four categories**: player kills, generator blocks mined, playtime and real-money store spending.
- **Claimable rewards**: each tier runs your own console commands when the player claims it.
- **Live progress**: players get a message and a sound the moment they reach a tier.
- **Fair play built in**: same-IP kills and repeat kills on the same victim don't count.
- **Clean seasons**: a reset wipes progress and claims but never counts old store orders again.
- **Native Folia support**, with PayNow requests off the game threads.
