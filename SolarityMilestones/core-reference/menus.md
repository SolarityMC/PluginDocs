`/milestones` opens the main menu. Nothing can be taken out of either menu.

## Main menu

One icon per category, in the order they're written in `config.yml`. Room for four. Each icon shows
the player's progress, the next tier, how many tiers they've claimed, and a line when rewards are
ready. An icon with rewards waiting glows. Click one to open the category.

## Category

The category icon at the top shows the player's progress, with up to 11 tiers below it:

- **Locked**: a grey dye with progress towards that tier.
- **Ready**: the category icon, glowing, with a gold star. Click it to claim the reward.
- **Claimed**: a lime pane with a tick.

A tier's `lore` from the config is shown on its icon above the status lines. The arrow at the bottom
goes back to the main menu.
