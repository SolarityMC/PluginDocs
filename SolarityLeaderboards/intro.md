SolarityLeaderboards is the leaderboard plugin for Solarity's GenSMP, for **Paper** and **Folia**
26.3. It shows six boards in a `/leaderboards` menu and through PlaceholderAPI. Version 26.11.0 is a
rebuild of SolarityLeaderboards 2.0.0 and reads its config and stats as they are.

## Key features

- **Six boards**: kills, deaths, blocks mined, gens sold, baltop and playtime.
- **A hub menu** with the top 3 of every board and your own rank, then pages of player heads.
- **Live counts**: kills, deaths and blocks count the moment they happen.
- **SolarityGens built in**: gen blocks mined and gens sold come straight from it.
- **Placeholders** for any position on any board, for holograms and scoreboards.
- **Native Folia support**, with saves off the game threads.
