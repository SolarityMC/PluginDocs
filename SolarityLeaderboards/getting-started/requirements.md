- **Java 25** or newer.
- **Paper or Folia 26.3** or newer (Canvas works too).

## Optional

| Plugin | What it adds |
|---|---|
| SolarityGens 26.11.0+ | Click-to-mine gens on the blocks board, and the gens sold board |
| Vault, with an economy plugin | The baltop board |
| SolarityEconomy | The baltop board, with every balance at once instead of the last one seen online |
| PlaceholderAPI | The `%solarityleaderboards_...%` placeholders |

A board whose source is missing stays empty; nothing else changes.
